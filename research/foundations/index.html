All Research Directions

Foundations of Generative Models

We study generative processes on continuous, discrete, and geometric spaces. Flow matching and flow maps provide tools for understanding how models move between probability distributions, and for making that transport more efficient.

Flow MatchingFlow MapsGeometry

Key Directions

Geometry and Discrete Data

Generative processes on manifolds and categorical probability spaces.

Flow Matching and Flow Maps

Probability paths, learned transport and generation in fewer steps.

Dynamics and Control

Non-gradient dynamics and methods for steering a generative process.
