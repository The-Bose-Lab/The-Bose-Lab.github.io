All Research Directions

AI for Science

We develop generative models for molecular systems, connecting methods in machine learning with protein design, molecular dynamics, and crystal structure prediction. Sequence, structure, and interactions provide complementary views of these systems.

Key Directions

Sequence and Structure

Joint modelling and design of biological sequences and molecular structures.

Molecular Interactions

Cofolding, molecular complexes, and structure-conditioned generation.

Materials and Dynamics

Generative modelling for molecular configurations and crystalline systems.
