All Research Directions

Neural Samplers & Boltzmann Generators

We develop learned samplers for distributions known through an unnormalized density or energy. This includes neural transport, annealing, iterated energy matching, and conditional generation.

Boltzmann GeneratorsAnnealingEnergies

Key Directions

Energy-Based Sampling

Learning from energies when normalized likelihoods are unavailable.

Annealing and Tempering

Connecting tractable distributions with more concentrated target distributions.

Structured Generators

Invertible and autoregressive constructions for molecular sampling.
