How to apply

01

PhD at Imperial

Apply through the Department of Computing admissions portal and name Joey Bose as your preferred supervisor. Funded positions are announced on the News page and on social media.

02

MSc & undergraduate projects

Imperial students can reach out directly by email with a CV, transcript, and a short note on which of our papers interests you and why.

03

Visitors & interns

We occasionally host visiting students and research interns. Strong candidates typically have prior research experience in generative modelling or a closely related area.

04

Before you write

Please read two or three of our representative papers first — a message that engages with the work goes to the top of the pile.
